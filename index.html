The Wheel of Pain

Four divers. Four stratagems each, no repeats within a squad member, though two divers may well be handed the same one. Primary, secondary, grenade and booster drawn from the entire armory, and since boosters do not stack, no two divers ever share one. Nothing is themed, nothing is balanced, and nothing is negotiable — though you may reroll any single line if the Wheel has been especially unkind. Click a diver's name to rename them; names stick in this browser.
